# Canvas O Coffee: Image Asset Requirements

Status: **Draft for owner review.** No image-dependent section will be built until this list
is approved and the Essential assets are supplied.
Last updated: 2026-11-23

Claude does not generate images. Every asset below is either **generated by you**
(AI image tool or illustrator), **photographed at the café**, or **supplied by the owner**.

---

## 1. Ground rules

1. **Truthfulness.** Images that show *the café itself* (interior, exterior, counter,
   gallery) must be **real photographs of Canvas O Coffee**. They are never generated.
   Generated images are used only as mood or illustrative imagery and are never captioned
   or described as "our café".
2. **No invented food details.** Generated food images show only the item named, with no
   sides, dips, garnishes or accompaniments the café has not confirmed.
3. **No text, logos or brands in any generated image.** No readable words on books,
   signage, cups or packaging. In particular, no Maggi or cola packaging or logos.
   All words on the site are real HTML text.
4. **No people's faces** in generated images. Real photos of staff or customers need their
   consent.
5. **No external hot-linked images.** Every image lives in this repository.
6. **The iced latte is not on the supplied menu.** It is brand storytelling only (see
   `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §7).

---

## 2. Global art direction (applies to every photographic asset)

Use this as the shared style suffix in every generation prompt so the set looks like one
shoot:

> Editorial still-life photography, warm natural window light from the left, deep soft
> shadows, rich coffee-brown and oxblood tones, dark walnut wooden surface, shallow depth of
> field, subtle film grain, realistic textures, restrained styling, no text, no logos,
> no people.

| Attribute | Direction |
|---|---|
| Light | One warm key light from the **left** (late-morning or golden-hour sun), shadows falling right. Keep the same direction in every image so they sit together. |
| Palette | Burgundy #461C25, walnut #30231D, parchment #E9DFC9, ivory #F5EBDD, brass #B08A4B accents. Green only from real leaves. |
| Surfaces | Dark walnut wood, aged paper, linen, matte stoneware, clear glass, small brass details |
| Grading | Warm and slightly muted. Blacks lifted to walnut, never pure black. No teal-orange, HDR or heavy vignette. |
| Avoid | Glossy stock-food look, artificial steam swirls, floating ingredients, overly saturated colour, neon, marble, hotel luxury, gold glitter |

Botanical assets use a separate illustration style (§5.2).

## 3. Folder structure and file naming

You supply **master files** here (exact filenames below):

```text
assets-src/images/
├── brand/            logo.svg (+ logo.png)
├── hero/             hero-coffee-desktop.jpg, hero-coffee-mobile.jpg
├── botanical/        coffee-branch-horizontal.png, coffee-branch-vertical.png,
│                     coffee-cherry-cluster.png, cherry-to-bean-sheet.png
├── signature/
│   └── iced-latte/   iced-latte-01-empty-glass.jpg … iced-latte-05-complete.jpg
├── story/            story-slow-moment.jpg, story-coffee-beans.jpg
├── menu/
│   ├── categories/   tea.jpg, coffee.jpg, momo.jpg, … mocktails.jpg
│   └── items/        {item-slug}.jpg   (optional real photos, see Appendix A)
├── cafe/             interior-wide.jpg, interior-table.jpg, storefront.jpg, counter.jpg
├── gallery/          gallery-01.jpg … gallery-09.jpg
├── reading/          reading-corner.jpg
└── textures/         parchment-tile.jpg
```

The build script (`npm run images`, Phase 2) converts each master into responsive files
that the code uses:

```text
public/images/<folder>/<name>-<width>.avif
public/images/<folder>/<name>-<width>.webp
public/images/<folder>/<name>-<largest>.jpg|png   (fallback)
```

Widths come from 640 / 1024 / 1600 / 2400 and are never upscaled. Example:
`assets-src/images/hero/hero-coffee-desktop.jpg` → `/images/hero/hero-coffee-desktop-1600.avif`.
Code always references assets by ID through a typed manifest (`src/data/images.ts`). A
missing asset shows a neutral "image pending" panel and never breaks the page.

**Master file rules:** sRGB colour. JPG quality 90 or better (≤ 5 MB) for opaque images,
PNG-24 with alpha (≤ 8 MB) for transparent ones. Equal or larger than the stated
resolution is fine. Smaller is not.

### 5.3 Signature iced-latte sequence

The visual centrepiece of the site and its single bold moment. A sticky frame cross-fades
through five images while the visitor scrolls past short step captions (HTML text).

| Frame | ID | File | What happens | Liquid level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IL-01 | `signature/iced-latte/iced-latte-01-empty-glass.jpg` | The empty, clean, dry glass. Nothing pouring. | 0% |
| 2 | IL-02 | `signature/iced-latte/iced-latte-02-milk-pour.jpg` | A thin, smooth stream of cold white milk pours in from the **top-right** (only the spout of a small steel pitcher visible at the frame edge). Gentle surface movement. | ~50% |
| 3 | IL-03 | `signature/iced-latte/iced-latte-03-espresso-pour.jpg` | A thin dark espresso stream from a small glass shot jug, **same entry point top-right**. Espresso blooms into the milk as soft marbled brown clouds in the upper half. | ~65% |
| 4 | IL-04 | `signature/iced-latte/iced-latte-04-ice-drop.jpg` | Three clear ice cubes: one in mid-air just above the rim, two breaking the surface with a small, restrained splash. Nothing pouring. | ~80% |
| 5 | IL-05 | `signature/iced-latte/iced-latte-05-complete.jpg` | The finished iced latte: 4–5 cubes settled, a soft gradient from milk-white at the bottom through caramel to coffee-brown at the top, light condensation on the outside of the glass | ~85% |

Shared specification for IL-01 to IL-05:

| Field | Specification |
|---|---|
| Section | Homepage → Signature Drink Experience |
| Style | Dark atmospheric product photography. Backdrop is a seamless dark walnut-to-oxblood gradient (#30231D → #461C25), slightly lighter directly behind the glass. Backlight from upper-left makes the liquid glow, with soft fill from the left and a dark right side. |
| Aspect / resolution | 4:5 · 1600 × 2000, **all five exactly the same pixel size** |
| Transparency | No (backdrop baked in; edges are feathered into the section background in CSS) |
| Type | **Sequence** (5 frames) |
| Device | Both (one set: beside the text on desktop, full-width sticky frame on mobile) |
| Priority | **Essential** (all five) |
| Variations | Exactly five. No more frames unless testing shows a jump. |
| Source | GEN (or a real tripod shoot) |
| Reuse | IL-05 is the **reduced-motion fallback** and the static image on very small or slow devices. All five appear as a thumbnail strip in the reduced-motion version. |

**Keeping the frames consistent.** Cross-fades only look like one continuous shot if
nothing moves except what is supposed to change.

1. **Locked camera.** Same focal length (~85 mm equivalent), height (slightly above the rim,
   about 5–10° downward so the liquid surface is just visible) and framing in all five.
2. **Locked glass.** A straight-sided, thick-based clear tumbler (~350 ml, no logo, no
   pattern), horizontally centred at **50%**. The base sits at **85% of frame height** and the
   rim at about **35%**, so there is headroom for the pour. Glass outline, reflections and
   highlights are identical in frames 1–4. Condensation appears **only in frame 5**.
3. **Locked set.** Same backdrop, the same tabletop edge in the bottom ~15%, same light
   positions and intensity. No props other than the pour vessels.
4. **One entry point.** Milk and espresso both enter from the top-right edge, so the
   eye stays on the same path. No hands.
5. **Only the glass interior and the pour path change.** Everything outside that region
   should be pixel-identical.
6. **Recommended production method (AI).** Generate **IL-01 first** as the master plate.
   Create IL-02 to IL-05 by **editing / inpainting IL-01** with a mask covering only the
   glass interior and the area above the rim, using the same seed and canvas size. Do not
   re-generate each frame from scratch; independent generations will not line up.
7. **Recommended production method (real shoot).** Tripod, manual exposure, white balance
   and focus all locked, remote shutter, glass position taped on the table.
8. **Check before delivery.** Stack the five frames as layers and toggle them. The glass
   must not shift or rescale. Claude will also run a pixel-difference check on arrival
   and report any drift.
